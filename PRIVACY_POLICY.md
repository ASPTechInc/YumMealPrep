# Privacy policy

Last updated: 28-10-2026

YumMealPrep is a free and open-source (FOSS) meal planner and recipe book built with Flutter.
This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## Summary

- YumMealPrep has no accounts, analytics, adverts or trackers.
- Your meals, recipes, meal plan, pictures and settings are stored only on your device.
- YumMealPrep does not send your data to its developers or to any server.
- On Android the app does not request the internet permission.

## Data stored on your device

YumMealPrep keeps the following in a database in the app's private storage:

- The meals and recipes you add or edit, including the sample ones
- Your meal plan
- Which meals and recipes are favourites
- Your theme choice (system, light or dark)

Pictures you add to a meal or recipe are copied into the app's private storage, resized and with
their metadata removed, including any location the photo was taken. The original picture in your
gallery or files is not changed. When you replace or remove a picture, or delete
the meal or recipe, the copy is deleted.

In the web version, the same data is stored in your browser's storage for this site.

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its data) deletes everything above.

## Backups

**Export data** saves your meals, recipes, meal plan and pictures to a single `.backup` file in a
place you choose. **Import data** reads a file you choose. YumMealPrep does not upload backups
anywhere. If you save a backup to a cloud folder, the provider of that service handles it under
its own privacy policy.

## Sharing and printing

**Share or print** creates a PDF of a meal or recipe on your device. It is only sent where you
choose, using your device's print or share options.

## Network connections

YumMealPrep does not make network connections of its own. The sample meals and recipes, the
fonts used in PDFs, and the licence, privacy and change log documents are bundled with the app.

The **Source code** and **Report a problem or idea** links open GitHub in your browser. What
happens there is covered by GitHub's privacy policy.

## Permissions

YumMealPrep does not request any special permissions. When you add a picture or choose a backup
file, your device's own file or photo picker opens and only the item you pick is shared with the
app.

## Open source

You can read the source code to check how YumMealPrep handles data:
https://github.com/ASPTechInc/YumMealPrep

## Changes to this policy

This policy is updated whenever the app's data handling changes. The latest version is in the
repository and in the app, under **Privacy policy** in the side menu.
