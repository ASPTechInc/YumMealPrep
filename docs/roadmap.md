# Roadmap

Ideas for future versions of YumMealPrep. They all have to work offline and without tracking.
Comment on or open an [issue](https://github.com/ASPTechInc/YumMealPrep/issues) if you would like
to help with one.

## Planned

- **Shopping list**: build a list from the ingredients of the recipes behind a week's meals.
- **Link meals to recipes**: show the recipes that make up a meal, such as Pelau with Coleslaw.
- **Copy a day or week** of the meal plan to another date.
- **Scale a recipe** to a different number of servings.
- **Import recipes** from other open formats, such as the JSON-LD `Recipe` data many recipe
  websites publish, or exports from other FOSS recipe apps.
- **Translations**, starting with the app's own text (meals and recipes are your own words).

## Maybe

- Optional sync between your own devices without a central server, for example through a folder
  synchronised with Syncthing.
- Nutrition values typed in as numbers, alongside the label photos.
- Reminders for planned meals, using local notifications only.
